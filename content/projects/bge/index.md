## Genomics for Europe's biodiversity

Biodiversity Genomics Europe (BGE) accelerates the use of genomic science to
understand biodiversity, monitor how it is changing, and support the
interventions that address its decline. It brought the DNA barcoding and
reference genome sequencing communities together across Europe, coordinated by
the Naturalis Biodiversity Center.

The original BGE project ran from September 2022 to February 2026, funded by
Horizon Europe under
[grant agreement 101059492](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101059492), with
33 partners across 20 countries. The work continues as
[BGE+](https://biodiversitygenomics.eu/) (grant agreement 101289347), which
connects iBOL Europe, ERGA and CETAF and describes itself as a first step
towards a European biodiversity genomics infrastructure.

## Galaxy's part in it

Galaxy provides the analysis side: accessible, scalable, reproducible genome
assembly and annotation, running on European public infrastructure rather than
on hardware each project has to find for itself.

- **Assembly and annotation workflows.** The assembly workflows developed with
  the [Vertebrate Genomes Project](/projects/vgp/) carry over directly to BGE
  species. See
  [Galaxy's contributions to genome assembly and annotation](/news/2025-03-18-assembly-galaxy/).
- **The ERGA Assembly Report.** Galaxy hosts the community-developed
  [ERGA Assembly Report](/news/2024-09-19-erga-ear/), a standard QC document for
  genome assemblies, and the
  [EAR Bot](/news/2026-03-07-erga-bot/) that automates its review.
- **Training.** BGE assembly and annotation
  [workshops](/news/2025-05-19-bge-workshops/) run on Galaxy with
  [Training Infrastructure as a Service](/eu/tiaas/) behind them.

Naturalis, the project coordinator, hosts its own Galaxy server.

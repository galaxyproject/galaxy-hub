## Analysis of NIH Common Fund data, on Galaxy

The CFDE Cloud Workspace gives researchers a place to work with
[NIH Common Fund](https://commonfund.nih.gov/dataecosystem) datasets: manage and
integrate data, draw on an established catalogue of bioinformatics tools, build
and share workflows, and collaborate with colleagues — with an initial tier that
costs the researcher nothing.

The analysis interface is Galaxy, so most life sciences researchers arrive
already knowing how to use it, and the tools and workflows the Galaxy community
has built are available from day one.

- Workspace: [cfdeworkspace.org](https://cfdeworkspace.org/)
- Galaxy instance: [galaxy.cfdeworkspace.org](https://galaxy.cfdeworkspace.org/)

## The project

The Galaxy teams at Penn State and Johns Hopkins Universities received a
five-year NIH award (#OT2OD037936) through the
[Common Fund Data Ecosystem](https://commonfund.nih.gov/dataecosystem) (CFDE) to
develop the **CFDE Cloud Workspace Implementation Center** (CWIC). The work is a
collaboration with the
[Texas Advanced Computing Center](https://tacc.utexas.edu/) (TACC) and the
[San Diego Supercomputer Center](https://www.sdsc.edu/) (SDSC), building on
Galaxy, the [CloudBank](https://www.cloudbank.org/) project, and TACC's compute
and storage.

Beyond the hosted Galaxy instance, the workspace offers access to TACC
high-performance computing and, through CloudBank, to commercial cloud
resources.

The choice of Galaxy as the foundation was deliberate: it runs across all major
cloud providers and academic compute resources, which gives the project cost
control and flexibility over time, and its open source licence means the
workspace can outlive the award.

See the [award announcement](/news/2024-11-27-tacc-sdsc-grant/), the
[TACC press release](https://tacc.utexas.edu/news/latest-news/2024/11/25/tacc-receives-nih-award-to-develop-cloud-workspace-implementation-center/),
and the [NIH CFDE Funded Research](https://commonfund.nih.gov/dataecosystem/FundedResearch)
page.

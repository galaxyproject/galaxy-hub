## Galaxy CoDex

The Galaxy Communities Dock (CoDex) is a catalogue of Galaxy resources — tools,
training material, and workflows — that can be filtered for any community. Each
community gets its own interactive tables, which can be embedded in a subdomain
or a community website.

The catalogues are assembled from the Galaxy Tool Shed, the Galaxy Training
Network, public Galaxy servers, and WorkflowHub, and are updated weekly. Tool
entries carry bio.tools and Conda status alongside an expert review field, so a
community can mark what it keeps and what it considers deprecated.

CoDex also holds the front page and tool panel content for community
[Galaxy Labs](/projects/labs/).

Any Galaxy community can be added. The Galaxy Training Network has a
[learning pathway for creating a SIG and its CoDex entry](https://training.galaxyproject.org/training-material/learning-pathways/sig-and-codex-creation.html),
and the [communities directory](https://github.com/galaxyproject/galaxy_codex/tree/main/communities)
in the repository documents what each community needs to provide.

## Galaxy on AnVIL

AnVIL — the NHGRI Genomic Data Science Analysis, Visualization, and Informatics
Lab-space — brings the analysis to the data rather than the data to the
researcher. Each researcher or group launches their own Galaxy instance inside
AnVIL, running on Google Cloud Platform alongside the large human genomic
datasets hosted there.

AnVIL is a FISMA-Moderate environment, so analyses can be run on
personally identifiable data. It brings together Terra, Gen3, Dockstore, Galaxy,
Bioconductor, and Jupyter in one platform, and is funded by NHGRI.

See also the [AnVIL platform entry](/use/anvil/) for quotas, support links, and
citation details.

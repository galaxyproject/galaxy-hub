## Galaxy Labs

A Galaxy Lab is a landing page tailored to one research domain: a front page, a
curated tool panel, and workflows relevant to that community, with buttons that
point at whichever Galaxy server the page is viewed on.

Labs are built by the communities themselves. A community keeps its Lab content
— a YAML root file plus Markdown or HTML sections — in a repository, and the
Galaxy Labs Engine renders it. Lab content for a number of communities lives in
[Galaxy CoDex](https://github.com/galaxyproject/galaxy_codex), and Labs are
served from Galaxy subdomains such as
[microbiology.usegalaxy.eu](https://microbiology.usegalaxy.eu/).

The Labs Engine is led by the [Galaxy Australia](/use/usegalaxy-au/) team. To
build a Lab, start with the generator at
[labs.usegalaxy.org.au](https://labs.usegalaxy.org.au/).

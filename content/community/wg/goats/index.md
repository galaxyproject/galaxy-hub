## What GOATS does

GOATS — **G**alaxy **O**utreach **A**nd **T**raining (and **S**upport) —
coordinates and guides Galaxy's outreach and training efforts, along with user,
admin and developer support and documentation.

It exists because those activities happen everywhere in Galaxy at once. Training
material, conference talks, newsletters, the help forum, community calls and
documentation are produced by many different people across many institutions and
projects. GOATS is where that work is coordinated globally rather than
duplicated locally.

The group was formed to take on exactly that coordination problem; Dave Clements
described the reasoning when he
[handed over Galaxy's community outreach in 2021](/news/2021-12-clements/).

## Scope

- **Outreach** — how Galaxy presents itself to new and existing users, and how
  the project shows up at conferences and in the wider community.
- **Training** — coordination across Galaxy's training efforts, most visibly the
  [Galaxy Training Network](https://training.galaxyproject.org/).
- **Support** — user, admin and developer support, including the
  [Galaxy Help forum](https://help.galaxyproject.org/).
- **Documentation** — making sure the project's documentation is written,
  current, and findable.

## What the group is working on

*As of November 2026.*

- **Conferences** — preparing for upcoming Galaxy conferences, and working with
  the organisers of the [Galaxy Community Conference](/events/gcc2027/) to help
  where the group can.
- **Keeping this Hub current** — galaxyproject.org is a community site, and
  keeping it accurate is ongoing work rather than a one-off. See
  [contributing to the Hub](/hub/contributing/).
- **Coordinating with the GTN** — in particular the annual
  [Galaxy Training Academy](https://training.galaxyproject.org/training-material/events/),
  the GTN's yearly global training event.

## Where GOATS sits

GOATS is one of Galaxy's [Working Groups](/community/wg/). Working Groups are
part of the [Galaxy Governance Model](/community/governance/) and are
represented by the [Galaxy Technical Board](/community/governance/gtb/).

Working Groups are distinct from [Special Interest Groups](/community/sig/):
a SIG gathers people around a scientific domain or region, while a Working Group
takes on a cross-cutting area of the project itself. GOATS works with SIGs
rather than replacing them — a SIG that needs training material or help reaching
its community is a natural GOATS collaborator.

## Meetings

GOATS meets weekly, and moves the time according to the week of the month.
Galaxy's outreach and training people are spread across the Americas, Europe and
Australia, and no single hour is reasonable for all three at once — so rather
than fixing one time and quietly excluding a third of the group, each week pairs
two regions at hours that work for both. **Everyone, wherever they are, gets at
least two calls a month at a sensible local hour.**

| Week of the month | Day | Western US | Eastern US | Central Europe | Australia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **First** — US + Europe | Wednesday | 8:00 a.m. | 11:00 a.m. | 5:00 p.m. | 1:00 a.m. Thu |
| **Second** — US + Australia | Monday / Tuesday | 4:00 p.m. Mon | 7:00 p.m. Mon | 1:00 a.m. Tue | 9:00 a.m. Tue |
| **Third** — US + Europe | Thursday | 6:00 a.m. | 9:00 a.m. | 3:00 p.m. | 11:00 p.m. |
| **Fourth** — Europe + Australia | Tuesday | 1:00 a.m. | 4:00 a.m. | 10:00 a.m. | 6:00 p.m. |

When a month runs to a fifth week, there is no call that week.

All of these are on the
[Galaxy Working Groups calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0?cid=NWw2bzBtc2ZkdW9pcjU5aHJhYjBqbGtvY2NAZ3JvdXAuY2FsZW5kYXIuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbQ),
which is the place to check if a daylight-saving change has shifted the times
relative to yours.

Each week is named for the two regions it is meant to suit; the other times are
shown so you can tell at a glance whether a given week's call is one you would
want to be awake for. You are welcome at any of them, not only your region's.

## Taking part

Everyone is welcome. Outreach, training and support need far more than
developers: writers, trainers, forum answerers, conference organisers, designers
and people who simply notice when something is confusing.

The best way to start is to join the Matrix channel and say hello.

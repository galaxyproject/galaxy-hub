# Terms of Service for the European Galaxy Server (2026 update)

Our priority has always been to enable science for all researchers. Every day, scientists across Europe and
beyond use the European Galaxy server to analyse data, build workflows, and
share results, and we want that to continue this.

But keeping this promise is becoming harder. Hardware prices are extremely
high at the moment, and the cost of storage and compute has not been falling
the way it used to. At the same time, abusive behaviour, increasingly enabled by
"AI" tooling, is a daily challenge for our administrators.
We are a small team operating on a best-effort basis, and we cannot let abuse take capacity away from legitimate science.

That is unfortunately why we need to act. Our new revised Terms are about protecting the Service so it stays
available for everyone who uses it responsibly. We are trying our best and ask for your understanding
and your help in keeping Galaxy Europe a shared resource for science.

---

The Freiburg Galaxy Team has published a **comprehensively revised set of Terms
of Service** for the European Galaxy server, covering
[usegalaxy.eu](https://usegalaxy.eu) and all `*.usegalaxy.eu` sub-sites.
The updated Terms, together with a revised [Privacy Policy](https://usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/privacy-policy)
and [GDPR rights](https://usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/gdpr-rights) page have taken effect on
**2026-09-07**.

**Please read the new Terms of Service at
[usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/tos](https://usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/tos).** By continuing to use
the Service after the effective date, you agree to the revised Terms. If you do
not agree, you may request deletion of your account and data as described on our [User Rights under the GDPR](https://usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/gdpr-rights)
page. Of course we always welcome feedback.

## What has changed

Please read the entire Terms of Service at
[usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/tos](https://usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/tos). A few important points below.

### Acceptable use

The new "Acceptable Use" section sets out, as an explicit list, what the
Service may and may not be used for. The Service is for science, research, and
education. Prohibited activities include running non-scientific cryptography
applications, processing data from illegitimate sources, using compute resources
solely to generate revenue, and any activity that constitutes a (cyber)crime.
You are also responsible for data you choose to make public via the Service's
sharing features.

### Accounts and account maintenance

You are now explicitly responsible for safeguarding your account credentials and
for all activity under your account. **You must keep your registered email
address valid and active**, because it is used together with Galaxy
notifications, for service notices, including inactivity warnings. If email to
your address bounces, it is treated as unreachable and the data retention steps
below may follow. You can update your email address under *User → User
Preferences*.

### Data retention

Data retention is a newly added section that formalises what happens to inactive accounts:

- **12 months** without login: we notify you by email and via Galaxy notifications;
  if you do not log in within **one month** of the notice, your account is **deactivated**
  (data remains stored for six more months but is no longer accessible).
- **6 months** after deactivation: your **user-generated data is deleted**. An
  account shell (username, email, analysis metadata) is retained and can be
  reactivated on request.
- **10 years** of total inactivity: the account record itself is **deleted**.

You remain responsible for transferring data you wish to archive or publish to
suitable public repositories, in line with the FAIR principles. Have a look at our dedicated [storage website](https://galaxyproject.org/eu/storage/) to learn more..

### Account suspension and automated measures

When our systems detect abusive behaviour, they may **temporarily disable
access automatically** to protect the Service.
You will be informed by email and may **request human review** by contacting
[contact@usegalaxy.eu](mailto:contact@usegalaxy.eu).

## What you need to do

1. **Read the new Terms of Service** at [usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/tos](https://usegalaxy.eu/gdpr/tos)
2. **Agree to them** when you want to use the Service in the future.
3. **Check that your registered email address is current** under *User → User
   Preferences*, so you receive important notices including inactivity warnings.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at
[contact@usegalaxy.eu](mailto:contact@usegalaxy.eu) or reach out on
[Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/#usegalaxy-eu_Lobby:gitter.im).

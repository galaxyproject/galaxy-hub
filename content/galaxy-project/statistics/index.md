This page contains statistics about the [Galaxy Project](/galaxy-project/).  This page is updated every January and July, or whenever the project has a grant due, or whenever an advisory board meeting is set to occur.

The text and tables on this page were refreshed in **November 2026**.  Most of the charts are snapshots of spreadsheets that have not been updated since early 2022; each one is labelled with the period it covers, and current figures are given alongside it.



# Galaxy Support: Help Forum & Matrix Chat

The Galaxy Help Forum, [help.galaxyproject.org](https://help.galaxyproject.org/), replaced the Galaxy BioStar site on November 29, 2018.  It was created as a brand new site, with none of the posts or user data from our old BioStar site copied over.

[![](/galaxy-project/statistics/discourse-page-views.svg)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vT_ydoBELTsh_TZqS_DVn5a4qGTxu5LQLWsHDll-jSvQQzMl79t3XKWsbYEEuyynsCWoTVj0KDBOjj6/pubchart?oid=2126198183&format=interactive)

**Average monthly page views** doubled over the course of 2020 and 2021, while the number of new questions and new answers held steady at roughly 75 and 150 per month.  Page views growing while questions and answers held steady suggests the site reached a critical mass of responses: when people visit, they are now much more likely to find that someone has already asked and answered their question.

*The chart above covers November 2018 through December 2021.*  Page view counts come from the forum's administrative dashboard and are not published, so this series has not been extended. ## Mailing Lists

Most of Galaxy's mailing list traffic has migrated to the help forum and to Matrix.  Galaxy-Announce (see below) is now the only [mailing list](/mailing-lists/) with regular traffic; Galaxy-Dev has gone quiet.  Threads per year, counted from the public [list archives](https://lists.galaxyproject.org/archives/):

| List | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [Galaxy-Announce](https://lists.galaxyproject.org/archives/list/galaxy-announce@lists.galaxyproject.org/) | 15 | 14 | 16 | 19 | 13 | 23 |
| [Galaxy-Dev](https://lists.galaxyproject.org/archives/list/galaxy-dev@lists.galaxyproject.org/) | 45 | 16 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 0 |

Here's the history:

* 2011/12: Galaxy-Announce list launched.
* 2012/02: Default mailing list for newly registered users on [usegalaxy.org](/main/) was switched from Galaxy-User to Galaxy-Announce in mid 2012/02. At the same time a link to the other lists was also added to the registration form.
* 2014/04: Most of Galaxy-User traffic moved to the [Galaxy BioStar online forum](https://biostar.usegalaxy.org).
* 2014/06: Galaxy-User mailing list disabled.  All new user support traffic now goes to Galaxy BioStar
* 2018/11: [Galaxy Help Forum launched](https://help.galaxyproject.org/), superseding Galaxy BioStar site
* 2023: Galaxy-Dev traffic drops to near zero; developer discussion is now on Matrix and GitHub

# Communication Channels

The project uses the Galaxy-Announce mailing list and social media to keep the community up to date on what's happening in the larger Galaxy community. Galaxy-Announce was launched in December 2011 and became the default mailing list for newly registered users on [usegalaxy.org](/main/) in February 2012.  The Galaxy project started [using Twitter](/galaxy-on-twitter/) on a regular basis in February 2011, and added Mastodon in December 2022 and Bluesky in October 2023.

# Galaxy Platforms

Galaxy is available in a number of ways.  While most [installations of Galaxy](/admin/get-galaxy/) are private, many groups support [Galaxy instances in many different forms](/use/), including publicly accessible servers, Galaxy instance that can be easily launched on research and commercial clouds, containers, and virtual machines.  Prior to 2019 we counted only publicly accessible servers.  Moving forward we are tracking many ways to use galaxy.

[![](/galaxy-project/statistics/galaxy-platforms.svg)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1czXV7tWPPjQQiB-uk0NYsqmC967deQ9OLue0ByXntbs/pubchart?oid=289204282&format=interactive)

*The chart ends in January 2022.*  Counting the entries in the [/use/](/use/) catalog today gives:

| Platform type | Count |
|---|---:|
| Public servers | 137 |
| Containers | 28 |
| Academic clouds | 10 |
| Commercial clouds | 6 |
| Virtual machines | 6 |

A further 39 public servers, 12 containers, 3 academic clouds, 2 commercial clouds and 4 virtual machines are listed as retired.  These counts are almost unchanged since January 2022, which reflects how little the catalog has been curated rather than a halt in the growth of Galaxy platforms.

# Events

As the [Galaxy events calendar and archive](/events/) show, the Galaxy community is globally active.  Events listed in the archive peaked at 186 in 2021, during the period when almost everything was online, and have settled at roughly 50 to 70 per year since:

| 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 151 | 170 | 186 | 92 | 45 | 51 | 71 | 50 |

[Galaxy Community Conferences](/gcc/) are the flagship event of the Galaxy community. GCC has met annually since 2010.  [Participation](/gcc/#conferences) ran above 200 every year from 2012 through 2021, peaking at 804 for the online BCC2020, and was between 130 and 155 for the in-person conferences in 2022, 2023 and 2024.

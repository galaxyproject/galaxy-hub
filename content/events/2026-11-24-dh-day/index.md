# The University of Freiburg's first Digital Humanities Day

Digital Humanities (DH) are becoming increasingly important in scientific practice.
But where can researchers start - where can students learn more about DH, and what resources exist at the University of Freiburg?
The DH lab at the University of Freiburg is organising the **university's first DH Day** on November 24 to answer those questions and give researchers and students better insights into DH.
We from the European Galaxy Team are glad to join!

# Meet Galaxy at the DH Day

Galaxy will feature as one of the university's DH services. We will host a booth (12:00-15:00) to introduce the analysis platform [Galaxy](https://usegalaxy.eu), its DH-specific features, and the EU project [AI4Social+](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101292886), in which Galaxy will support AI readiness in the Social Sciences and Humanities. 
You can see the whole [program and more info here](https://dhlab.uni-freiburg.de/dhday2026/). Join us at Aula in KGI to learn more.

# GCC2027 Sponsors

<div class="alert alert-info" role="alert">
We're currently looking for GCC2027 sponsors. Sponsors will be listed here as they join.
</div>

The **Galaxy Community Conference (GCC)** is the flagship event for one of the
most impactful open-source platforms in computational biology. By becoming a
GCC2027 sponsor, you'll be investing in a global movement democratizing
data-intensive research.

GCC2027 brings together an audience of **200+ professionals**, including faculty,
students, postdocs, developers, eResearch infrastructure managers, and research
platform users. Hosting the conference in Canada is also an opportunity to
highlight [UseGalaxy Canada](https://usegalaxy.ca/) and strengthen connections
between Canadian researchers and the global Galaxy community.

## Sponsorship packages

The following packages are open to sponsors. All prices are in Canadian dollars, GST included.

### Platinum: $10,000 CAD

- Featured prominently in all event marketing and communication materials
- Sponsor of the conference dinner
- An invited presentation session
- Exhibit space
- Branding on the event website and social media posts
- 3 VIP in-person event passes and 4 virtual event passes
- Visibility at the [Galaxy Training Academy](https://training.galaxyproject.org/training-material/events/), a global online event with over 3,500 registrants from 122 countries

### Gold: $7,000 CAD

- Branding on the event website and social media
- A poster presentation slot and exhibit space
- Distribution of your marketing materials
- Sponsor of a lunch
- 1 VIP in-person event pass and 3 virtual event passes

### Silver: $4,000 CAD

- Branding on the event website and social media
- A poster presentation slot and exhibit space
- 1 VIP in-person event pass and 1 virtual event pass

### Bronze: $2,500 CAD

- Branding on the event website and social media
- A poster presentation slot and 1 VIP in-person event pass, **or** sponsor a break

### Friends of GCC2027: $1,000 CAD

- Branding on the event website

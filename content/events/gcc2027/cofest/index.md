## What is CoFest?

CoFest is two days of hands-on, collaborative work that follows the Galaxy
Community Conference. Participants team up on projects that make Galaxy better:
wrapping tools, building workflows, writing training material, improving the
user interface and the code base, and growing the community itself.

<div class="callout text-center">
    The goals of CoFest are to expand the Galaxy contributor community and its ecosystem.
</div>

* **Everyone's welcome.** You don't need to be a developer, and newcomers are especially encouraged to join.
* **Work in project teams.** Each project has a lead and real, achievable goals, and you're free to switch teams or join more than one.
* **No rigid agenda.** You don't need to know anyone or arrive with a finished plan. Just show up, find your people, and leave having built something real together.

## CoFest through the years

CoFest grew out of the GCC coding hackathons, first held at
[GCC2014](/events/gcc2014/hackathon/), and has become a GCC tradition.
At [GCC2026](/events/gcc2026/cofest/) in Clermont-Ferrand, participants worked on
19 projects, from AI agents and Kubernetes to new training materials and ecology
tools.

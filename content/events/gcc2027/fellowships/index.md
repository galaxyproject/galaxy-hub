# GCC2027 Fellowships

Financial support shouldn't be a barrier to joining us. GCC2027 fellowships
support students, early-career researchers, and community members whose
attendance depends on it, whether in person in the Montréal area or online.

Deadlines are [End of Day, Anywhere on Earth time](https://time.is/Anywhere_on_Earth).

## Before you apply

The [scholarships site](https://conferences.galaxyproject.org/scholarships) has the
full details on eligibility, what's covered, and what we ask of recipients, so
please read it before applying. You sign in with your
[ORCID iD](https://orcid.org/) to start an application.

## Questions?

Please reach out to the organizers at [gcc-org@lists.galaxyproject.org](mailto:gcc-org@lists.galaxyproject.org).

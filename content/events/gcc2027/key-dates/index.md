# GCC2027 Key Dates

Keep the following important dates in mind for GCC2027! These dates can save you
money and give you an opportunity to present your work.

<div class="gx-dates" style="max-width: 80%; min-width: 300px; margin: 0 auto;">

| Date | | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November 18, 2026 | 🟢 | 🎓 [Fellowship](/events/gcc2027/fellowships/) applications open |
| November 18, 2026 | 🟢 | 🎟️ [Registration](/events/gcc2027/register/) and 📝 [abstract submission](/events/gcc2027/abstracts/) open |
| February 15, 2027 | 🔴 | 🎓 Fellowship applications due for the first review round |
| February 28, 2027 | 🔴 | 🎟️ Early bird registration closes |
| March 15, 2027 | 🔴 | 🎓 Fellowship applications close (second review round) |
| March 25, 2027 | 🏆 | 🎓 Fellowship awards announced |
| April 15, 2027 | 🔴 | 📝 Abstract submissions for talks close |
| May 14, 2027 | 🔴 | 🎟️ Regular registration closes |
| May 17, 2027 | 🔴 | 📝 Abstract submissions for posters & demos close |
| June 13, 2027 | 🔴 | 🎟️ Late registration closes |
| **June 26, 2027** | 🎉 | 🧑‍🏫 [**GCC2027 Training Day**](/events/gcc2027/training/) |
| **June 27–29, 2027** | 🎉 | 🎤 [**GCC2027 Main Conference**](/events/gcc2027/schedule/) |
| **June 30 – July 1, 2027** | 🎉 | 🤝 [**GCC2027 CoFest**](/events/gcc2027/cofest/) |

</div>

Note that all deadlines are at midnight (end of the day) [Montréal time](https://time.is/Montreal) (Eastern Time).

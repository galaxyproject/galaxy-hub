# BoFs: Birds of a Feather

BoFs are a great opportunity to gather with other Galaxy community members around
shared topics, ideas, questions, or challenges. The sessions can be structured in
whatever way works best for the topic, whether that includes short presentations,
a guided discussion, or an open conversation.

Details on proposing a BoF for GCC2027 will be posted here.

The **ESG4Stars** project will see its kick-off meeting in the week of 5 October 2026.

The [EU-funded project](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101290798) brings together 13 European partners to work on sustainable scientific workflows that reduce the environmental footprint of scientific computing and data analysis on research infrastructure platforms like Galaxy.


# Meeting and Satellite Events

The kickoff meeting will take place at the University of Freiburg, Germany, that also acts as the coordinator of the project.

The meeting itself will be running for two full days on 5 and 6 October 2026 and will comprise:

- an overview of the project structure and goals
- presentations of the different work packages by their leads
- guest presentations from related projects and initiatives
- discussion sessions

It will be followed by **satellite activities** (hackathons, trainings and workshops) on the subsequent three days until the early afternoon of Friday, 9 October, to let you maximize the value of your travel to Freiburg and to get work on the project going immediately.

*A detailed agenda of the kickoff meetings and a list of satellite events will be published here closer to the event date.*

# Sustainable Travel

In line with the environmental focus of ESG4Stars, we would like to encourage participants to use public transport where possible.

## By Train

The main station is called Freiburg (Breisgau) Hbf and can be reached conveniently by long-distance (ICE) trains from the north (via Frankfurt, Mannheim and Karlsruhe) and the south (via Basel).
Regional trains connect the city also to the TGV station in Mulhouse, France.

For detailed connection information and booking you can check out one of these sites:

- [Deutsche Bahn](https://www.bahn.com/en)
- [Trainline](https://www.thetrainline.com/)
- [Railfinder](https://www.railfinder.eu/) *beta version*

or the website of your national railways operator(s).

If you need additional help for navigating through the European railways jungle, or if you should be worried by rumors of unreliable German train connections (these are only partly exaggerations ;-)), just contact us. We're happy to help.

## By Bus

There is a [FlixBus](https://global.flixbus.com/) stop directly at the train main station of Freiburg serving many European destinations.

## By Plane

If flying really is your only option, try to book a *direct* flight (to Frankfurt or to the Basel/Mulhouse/Freiburg EuroAirport) to minimize emissions and use public transport for your way to and from the airport.

## In Freiburg

To reach the meeting venue from the main train station, you have these two public transport options:
- local train: take the Breisgau S-Bahn (direction to Breisach) to the stop "Freiburg Messe/Universität"
- tram: use tram line 4 (direction to Messe) to the stop "Technische Fakultät" (see [tram plan](http://www.vag-freiburg.de/fahrplan-linien/netzplaene/liniennetzplan.html))

The trams are also the easiest way to get around in Freiburg during the week.

[Here](https://www.vag-freiburg.de/tickets) you can find the types of tram tickets that VAG offers (in German only, unfortunately).
The [2x4 rides ticket](https://www.vag-freiburg.de/tickets/einzel-und-tageskarten/2x4-fahrtenkarte) is often a good option for several days and/or passengers. You're getting two printed tickets, each for 4 rides on any date you like. You can use multiple trams/buses on one ride in a given direction and you need to stamp your ticket after boarding the first tram/bus of the ride. Within the city limits of Freiburg you only need the 1-zone (Preisstufe 1) version of the ticket.

Freiburg is also a great city to get around by bicycle. Take a look at the city's [Frelo bike sharing system](https://www.frelo.de/en/) if you're interested.

Again, please don't hesitate to ask us for help if you need it.

# Data analysis made easy - meet Galaxy

 "Enjoy our coffee & stay up to date ... in 15 minutes" is the motto of the University of Freiburg library's [coffee lecture series](https://www.ub.uni-freiburg.de/lernen-arbeiten/einfuehrungen-und-kurse/coffee-lectures/).
 We are excited to join the lecture series during the winter term and introduce Galaxy as a data analysis platform.

You want to analyse your data, text or images digitally, but do not know how? 
You only have a laptop but no programming skills or money for expensive programs? 
Do you want to consider good research data management, but do not know how? 
**Galaxy is here for you!**
Our on-site coffee lecture on **02.12.2026, 13:00** at the Freiburg University Library gives you a quick overview of how you can use the open source platform Galaxy (usegalaxy.eu) for your research. 
Are you already busy that day? Do not worry, there will be another coffee lecture introducing Galaxy on 20.01.2027 also at 13:00.

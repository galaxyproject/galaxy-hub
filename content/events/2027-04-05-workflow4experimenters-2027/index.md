Analyze your data with Galaxy and the Workflow4Metabolomics infrastructure! During this one-week-long on-site course (preceded by half-a-week of webinars), you will learn how to use the W4M-promoted Galaxy tools for metabolomics, and analyze your own LC-MS, GC-MS or NMR data through tutoring sessions.

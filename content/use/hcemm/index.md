comments:
  - >-
    HCEMM Galaxy is operated by the Scientific Computing
    facility of the Hungarian Centre of Excellence
    for Molecular Medicine (HCEMM), Szeged, Hungary.
  - >-
    The platform provides reproducible biomedical data
    analysis using Galaxy, with computational workloads
    dispatched through Pulsar to the institutional
    Slurm high-performance computing infrastructure.
  - >-
    The service supports computational biology research
    and is being expanded with workflows for genomics,
    transcriptomics, epigenetics, microbiome research,
    and other biomedical applications.
  - >-
    The web interface is publicly accessible. User
    accounts are available by invitation or approval
    for HCEMM researchers and authorised collaborators.
